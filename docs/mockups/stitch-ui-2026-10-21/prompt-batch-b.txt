# YSBK — Thư viện ký ức
## Intent
A distinctive Vietnamese student book-club website, vintage library catalogue meets independent literary magazine. Warm, youthful, quietly nostalgic, tactile paper, confident editorial hierarchy. Not an ecommerce shop and not a generic SaaS dashboard.
Exact brand: Câu lạc bộ Yêu Sách Bách Khoa Hà Nội. Slogan: Mở sách, đọc tương lai. Founded 16/09/2013.

## Typography
Explore NEWSREADER editorial serif for expressive large titles and quotes, BE VIETNAM PRO readable body/forms/navigation. Correct Vietnamese glyphs and diacritics. Body16px/1.6, UI14–16px, captions14px, desktop titles48–64px, mobile32–40px. Avoid all-caps paragraph copy. Use italic serif sparingly, not cursive UI. Tabular numbers in internal tables.
## Physical UI language
Library catalogue divider tabs with straight base and lightly rounded top corners; narrow double-rule editorial headers; small catalogue serials as decoration, no fake statistics.
Buttons rectangular2–4px radius, 1px ink edge, 2px lower offset shadow like a card pressed onto paper, primary evergreen+ivory label. Secondary ivory+green border; text links underlined. Distinct hover, pressed, disabled, loading, keyboard focus. Destructive terracotta+explicit words, no icon-only ambiguity.
Paper panels light 1px borders, 0 2px 0 border-color offset plus 0 8px 24px ink at8% opacity; overlay0 16px 48px ink at16%. Fine ruled lists, bookplates, date tickets, subtle bookmark notch. Plenty of clean whitespace. No aggressive grain, glass, neon, gradient blobs, neumorphism, pill-everything or giant rounded dashboard cards.
## Public IA
Approved landing remains a large central physical book that opens with scrolling; do not redesign the approved cover illustration. Story exits into borrowing guide and unified public hub.
ONE hub with6sections: Tra cứu sách / Đặt giữ chỗ / Tra cứu đơn / Lịch trực & sự kiện / Góc review / Tuyển thành viên. Keep navigation practical and show all routes via mobile menu.
Catalogue search title/author, category, availability,50books/page with pagination. Book detail summary, copies, deposit, reserve. Reservation no login: one book, name, phone, pickup session, CAPTCHA. Never expose technical IP/quota rules in user-facing UI.
Duty Tuesday/Thursday. Public reservation is a hold until end of pickup duty; no automatic shipping, checkout or buying.
## Internal IA
Tổng quan, Quầy mượn–trả, Kho sách, Lịch trực, Thành viên, Tuyển thành viên, Sự kiện, Bài viết, Quỹ & báo cáo, Quản trị & nội dung.
Clear loans/returns/deposits/alerts. Managers alone publish books/covers/summaries, duty/closure dates, events, recruitment. Distinguish draft vs published.
Collaborators1month, manual promotion; members become alumni. No redesign of backend policies.
